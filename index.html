What I fix

Common problems, sorted out

Downrigger keeps tripping the fuse

Often old, corroded, undersized wire. I replace it with heavy 8AWG so it stops cutting out.

Battery won't hold a charge

Finding the cause: parasitic drains, charging problems from the alternator, or faulty wiring.

New system installs

Building new electrical panels from scratch, with clean wire routing, labelling, and testing, so everything is easy to service later.

Adding solar

Solar installation and integration with your existing system so it actually keeps your batteries topped up.

Lights, pumps, or gear dead for no clear reason

Troubleshooting that finds the root cause instead of replacing parts until something works.

Messy or worrying wiring

Rewiring and tidy-up: proper fusing, solid connections, and wiring that's labelled and secured.

Also: DC and AC systems, shore power, and lithium battery upgrades (LiFePO4). If it's electrical and on a boat, ask.

About

I'm an electrical engineer by training, and I've been in the marine industry since 2023. I started Galactic Boatworks to give Campbell River boaters a straightforward alternative: someone who shows up, finds the real problem, fixes it properly, and charges a fair price.

I live aboard, so I know firsthand how much it matters when the electrical system on a boat just works.
