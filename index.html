About

Hi, I'm Joe

I'm an electrical engineer by training, and I've spent years building and working on boats. I started Galactic Boatworks to give Campbell River boaters a straightforward alternative: someone who shows up, finds the real problem, fixes it properly, and charges a fair price.

I live aboard, so I know firsthand how much it matters when the electrical system on a boat just works.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to bring my boat to you?

No. I work at your boat, whether it's in the water at the marina, on a trailer, or on the hard.

What areas do you cover?

Campbell River with no travel fee. I also travel to surrounding areas; a travel fee applies. Message me with your location and I'll let you know.

Will you give me a price before starting?

Yes. Small jobs get a quick estimate by text, and bigger projects get a written, itemized quote.

Can you work with parts I've already bought?

Often, yes. Let me know what you have and I'll tell you honestly whether it suits the job.

What kinds of boats do you work on?

Fishing boats, sailboats, powerboats, and aluminum builds. If you're not sure, just ask.

Got an electrical problem or project?
